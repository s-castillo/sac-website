About us

Care built around dignity, trust, and independence.

Finding care for someone you love is a big decision. We make it easier with dependable support built around their routines, comfort, and independence.

Whether you need a few hours or ongoing help, someone reliable is there when you cannot be, with respect for each person’s preferences, privacy, and dignity.

Our mission

San Antonio Caregivers exists to help individuals remain safe, comfortable, connected, and as independent as possible in the place they know best, while giving families a dependable source of support when they cannot do everything alone.

Dependable, compassionate, non-medical in-home care that supports safety, dignity, and independence.

Personal assistance services with integrity, professionalism, and respect, held to high standards of care, compliance, and accountability.

Thoughtful growth and strong operational foundations to be a trusted partner for families and a reliable community resource.

Our guiding principles

Five things we hold ourselves to.

Compassion

Every client is treated with genuine care, warmth, patience, and understanding.

Dependability

Families can count on us to communicate, show up, and follow through.

Dignity

We honor the individuality, privacy, preferences, and autonomy of each person we serve.

Integrity

We operate with honesty, professionalism, and respect at every level.

Accountability

We hold ourselves responsible for the quality, safety, and professionalism of the support we provide.

Professional care standards

Compassion matters. So do professionalism, accountability, and trust.

Inviting someone into your home requires trust. Our caregivers follow professional standards that protect the client, the family, and the caregiving relationship.

Professional Boundaries

Respectful, professional relationships with clients and families.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Client and care-related information stays private and confidential.

Documented Care

Services and relevant observations are documented every shift, per company procedures.

Training & Safety

Training in client safety, infection control, emergency procedures, confidentiality, and professional boundaries.

Agency Accountability

Caregivers answer to the agency and report client concerns, safety issues, incidents, and meaningful changes in care.

Caregiver-Client Matching

Assignments weigh the client’s needs against the caregiver’s skills, abilities, and suitability.
