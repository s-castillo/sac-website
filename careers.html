Why work with us

Why work with San Antonio Caregivers?

Meaningful One-on-One Work

Build relationships and make a practical difference in clients’ routines, comfort, and independence.

Caregiver-Client Matching

Assignments match client needs with your skills, abilities, and suitability.

Ongoing Training

Education and training opportunities throughout employment.

Support When You Need It

Stay connected for scheduling questions, client concerns, emergencies, and changes in care.

A Culture Built on Respect

Treat clients with dignity, and expect dignity and professionalism in return.

Who may be a good fit

  • Compassionate and patient
  • Dependable and punctual
  • Respectful of client routines and preferences
  • Comfortable working independently
  • A thoughtful communicator
  • Attentive to detail
  • Committed to client dignity and safety
  • Able to follow care plans, company procedures, and professional boundaries
  • Comfortable communicating with the office when questions or changes arise

What caregivers may help with

  • Companionship
  • Light housekeeping
  • Meal preparation
  • Transportation and errands
  • Personal assistance services
  • Respite support
  • Routine daily support based on the client’s service plan

Details on current openings, pay, scheduling, classification, benefits, and requirements will be added once confirmed. Contact us for current opportunities.
