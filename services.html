Respite care

A break for the people who care.

Temporary, dependable support for family caregivers who need time to rest, work, run errands, attend appointments, or simply recharge.

Respite care may be helpful when:

  • A family caregiver needs a few hours away
  • Work or family responsibilities make it difficult to be present
  • A loved one should not be left alone for an extended period
  • A family needs temporary support during a particularly demanding period

Our care approach

Care that fits the person, not the other way around.

We start with the person, their routines, and where help would make life safer, easier, or more comfortable.

Personalized Support

We learn each client’s routines, preferences, abilities, schedule, and comfort level, then plan care around the person.

Supporting Independence

Caregivers assist, not take over. Clients stay involved in their routines to keep confidence and independence.

Dignity in Personal Care

Hands-on help with privacy, patience, and respect: bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, mobility, transferring, and other daily activities in the service plan.

Companionship & Everyday Support

Conversation, hobbies, meals, light housekeeping, transportation, errands, medication reminders as permitted, and other non-medical support in the service plan.

Respite for Family Caregivers

A dependable way to step away, occasionally or on an ongoing schedule, depending on needs and availability.

Ongoing Communication

Needs change. Stay in touch so questions, scheduling concerns, and changes in care can be addressed.

Support During Changing Care Needs

Extra non-medical support when a loved one is living with dementia, has changes in memory or behavior, or receives hospice services from another provider. When appropriate to the service plan, we provide companionship and personal assistance within the family’s established care routine.
