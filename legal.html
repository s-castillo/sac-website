Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

San Antonio Caregivers LLC (“SAC,” “we,” or “us”) respects the privacy of people who visit our website, ask about care, or contact us about working with SAC. This policy describes how we handle information collected through our public website.

Information you provide

For care inquiries, we collect the contact details you provide and any details you choose to share about who needs care, the city or ZIP code, your preferred contact method, the type of help you are looking for, and the schedule you have in mind.

For caregiver applications, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and any information you choose to provide about your experience, availability, or interest in caregiving.

If you send another message through the website, we receive the information you choose to include. Please keep public form submissions brief and leave out medical records, diagnoses, Social Security numbers, financial information, and identity documents. If sensitive information is needed, contact SAC for instructions on how to provide it.

Information collected when you visit

Some technical information is collected automatically when you visit, so the site can load and stay secure. We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking tools to this website, and the site’s own code does not set cookies.

  • Hosting records. Our website host, GoDaddy, keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, the date and time of your visit, the pages and files you request, and your browser and device type. These records help keep the site running and secure.
  • Performance monitoring. Our host also runs a performance-monitoring script that measures how quickly pages load. It is set up by the host, not by SAC.
  • Fonts. The site’s typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address and browser details with that request.

Our host and these services may use limited technical data of their own, under their own privacy practices. You can limit or block cookies and scripts in your browser settings, and the pages and forms should continue to work.

How we use information

We use care inquiry information to respond, discuss the support you are looking for, and explain next steps. We use caregiver application information to review your interest in working with SAC and follow up about hiring. We may also use information to operate and protect the website, maintain records, and meet legal obligations.

When we share information

Information may be accessed by SAC staff who need it to respond to your inquiry or carry out their responsibilities.

We rely on a few service providers to run the website and deliver your messages. They receive information only for these purposes:

  • Website hosting. GoDaddy hosts the website and keeps the server records described above.
  • Form delivery. Formspree processes submissions from our care inquiry and caregiver application forms and delivers them to SAC by email.
  • Email. SAC receives and replies to messages using Google’s Gmail service.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information when authorized by you or when permitted or required by law.

Care records and employment records

If you arrange care with SAC, we provide information about client rights and the handling of care records through our admissions process. Formal hiring and employment records are handled through SAC’s hiring and personnel processes. If a background check is requested, SAC provides separate disclosures and requests authorization through the hiring process.

Retention and privacy requests

We keep website inquiries and initial applications only as long as needed to respond to you, follow up about care or hiring, keep reasonable business records, and meet legal requirements. If you become a client or are hired, your information becomes part of the care or employment records described above and is kept under those processes.

To ask about how we handle your information, request an update, or exercise an applicable privacy right, contact us at 726-500-3050 or sacaregiversllc@gmail.com. Please leave sensitive details out of your initial message. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request involving personal records.

If privacy laws that apply to SAC give you additional rights, such as access to or correction of your information, we will honor them and respond within the time the law requires.

Updates to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here and update the effective date.

Terms of Service

Effective November 18, 2026

These terms apply to your use of the public website operated by San Antonio Caregivers LLC (“SAC”). The website provides information about our non-medical in-home assistance services and ways to contact us about care or employment.

Care inquiries and caregiver applications

Submitting a care inquiry asks SAC to contact you. Services, schedules, fees, and responsibilities are established through a separate written service agreement.

Submitting a caregiver application expresses interest in employment. Any offer and related terms are communicated separately through SAC’s hiring process.

Using this website

Use the site lawfully and provide accurate information when contacting us. Share someone else’s information only when you have permission or legal authority to do so. Do not impersonate another person, attempt to access restricted information, or interfere with the website.

See our Privacy Policy for information about how we handle information submitted through the site.

Medical questions and emergencies

The website provides general information about non-medical support. For medical questions, contact a qualified health care professional. For an emergency, call 911. Website forms are intended for routine inquiries.

Links to other websites

Some links may take you to websites operated by other organizations. Those organizations set their own terms and privacy practices.

Questions and updates

Contact SAC at 726-500-3050 or sacaregiversllc@gmail.com with questions about these terms. If we update them, we will post the revised version here and update the effective date.

Accessibility

San Antonio Caregivers wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or magnification. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we review the site whenever we make changes.

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or you would like information in another format, please call 726-500-3050 or email sacaregiversllc@gmail.com. Tell us which page you were on and what got in the way, and we will help you directly and work to fix the problem.
